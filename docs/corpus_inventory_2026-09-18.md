Generated by `PYTHONPATH=. python3 scripts/corpus_inventory.py`. Re-run it rather
than trusting these numbers after the next collection.

Two things to read carefully before quoting any of it:

* **File level and deduplicated differ by a third.** 10,840 parquet files hold
  6,602 distinct `(run_id, gpu_uuid)` streams, because the same stream is written
  into more than one directory and `load_all_data` collapses those on
  `(ts, run_id, gpu_uuid)`. The deduplicated block is the corpus. 90,040
  file-level stream-minutes are really 59,657.
* **Stream-minutes are not wall-clock.** A 16-chip TPU cell running for 10
  minutes contributes 160 stream-minutes. Across distinct runs the corpus is
  325 wall-clock hours; as per-device telemetry it is 994 stream-hours.
